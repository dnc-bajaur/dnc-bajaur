Privacy Notice

District Nerve Center — Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan

The District Nerve Center is the software the district administration of Bajaur uses to record emergencies, route them to the department responsible, and track whether they were acted on. This notice describes what it holds, why, and who can see it.

Who is responsible

The Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur. The system belongs to the office, not to any individual officer, and responsibility for it stays with the office when postings change.

What is recorded

  • Emergency reports — what was reported, its category and assessed severity, when it happened and when it was recorded, and where, including a location captured from the reporting device where the person allowed it.
  • Contact details given by a reporter, where they choose to give them, so that the responding department can call back.
  • The district's own officers — name, post, department and official contact number, taken from the district's directory, so that an emergency can be routed to whoever holds a post today.
  • What was done — who was told, when, whether they acknowledged, what action was logged, and how the incident was closed. Photographs and files attached to an incident by responders are held with it.
  • Message delivery state — for WhatsApp notifications, whether a message was accepted, delivered or failed, and any reply sent back to the district's number.

Why

To coordinate emergency response across the district and to keep an accountable record of it. The district administration is empowered to record, hold, act on and respond to emergencies within the district. Nothing here is collected for advertising, and none of it is sold, rented, or shared for marketing.

WhatsApp

The district notifies its officers over WhatsApp, from one number the district controls, using Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta processes those messages in order to deliver them and reports back their delivery state. Only what is necessary to send a notification travels this way — the recipient's number and the message itself. Read receipts are not used to decide whether anybody acted; only a deliberate acknowledgement counts.

Who can see it

The district's control room, and officers of the district administration acting in their posts. Access follows the post a person holds, not the person, and it ends when they stop holding it. Every consequential action is attributable to the seat that performed it.

How long it is kept

Emergency records are retained indefinitely at present. The district has not yet set a retention period, and this notice will be updated to state one when it does, rather than implying a limit that is not in force.

Where it is held

On a server operated for the district, reachable only over an encrypted connection. A nightly backup is encrypted before it leaves that machine.

Requesting deletion, or asking a question

Write to dc-office@example.com, or to the Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur. Include enough detail to identify the record. Requests are answered by the office; note that records forming part of the district's account of an emergency response may be retained where there is a legal reason to keep them.
