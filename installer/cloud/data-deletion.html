Deleting your data

District Nerve Center — Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan

This page explains how to ask the district to delete information it holds about you, and what happens when you do. It sits beside the privacy notice, which describes what is held and why.

There is no Facebook sign-in to disconnect

Nobody signs in to this system with a Facebook, Instagram or other Meta account, and this system has never asked for one. The only Meta service it uses is the WhatsApp Business Platform, and it uses it in one direction: to send notifications about emergencies to the district's own officers, from one number the district controls, and to receive their replies. So there is no login to revoke and no Meta profile data to remove — what can be deleted is described below.

What can be deleted

  • Your contact details — a name, post, department and telephone number held in the district's directory so an emergency can be routed to whoever holds a post today, or so a responding department can call a reporter back.
  • Messages sent to you, and your replies — the WhatsApp notifications the district sent to your number, their delivery state, and anything you sent back to the district's number.
  • Contact details you gave when reporting an emergency, where you chose to give them.

How to ask

  1. Write to dc-office@example.com with the subject Data deletion request, or write to the Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur.
  2. Say which telephone number or which record the request is about. Without enough detail to identify it, the office cannot act on the request.
  3. The office answers the request. Where something cannot be deleted, you are told which part and why, rather than the request being closed silently.

There is no self-service button for this, and that is deliberate: this is an emergency record belonging to a government office, and deleting part of it is an act a person in that office takes and is accountable for.

What cannot be deleted, and why

Records that form part of the district's account of an emergency response may be retained where there is a legal reason to keep them. What was reported, when, who was told, and whether it was acted on is the district's own record of an emergency, and it is the thing this system exists to keep. The district will say when this applies to your request.

How long data is kept otherwise

Emergency records are retained indefinitely at present. The district has not yet set a retention period, and this page and the privacy notice will be updated to state one when it does, rather than implying a limit that is not in force.

Asking a question instead

The same address answers questions about what is held and why: dc-office@example.com, or the Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur.
