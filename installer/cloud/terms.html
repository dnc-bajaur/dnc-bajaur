Terms of Use

District Nerve Center — Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan

What this system is

The District Nerve Center is internal software of the district administration of Bajaur. It records emergencies, routes them to the department responsible, and keeps an accountable record of what was done. It is not a public service and does not accept reports from the general public.

Who may use it

Only the district's control room and officers authorised by the Office of the Deputy Commissioner. Access follows the post a person holds, not the person, and it ends when they stop holding it. Accounts are issued and revoked by the district administration.

How it must be used

Records entered here form the district's account of an emergency response. Entries must be accurate and made in good faith. Credentials are not to be shared, and the system is not to be used for any purpose other than district operations. Every consequential action is attributable to the seat that performed it and is retained in an append-only log.

Notifications

The system notifies officers over WhatsApp from a number the district controls, and records whether each message was delivered and whether it was acknowledged. Officers are expected to acknowledge notifications they receive. An acknowledgement is a deliberate act; a message being read is not treated as one.

Availability

The district administration operates this system for its own use and makes no warranty of uninterrupted availability. It is provided to authorised users as-is, for official purposes.

Contact

Office of the Deputy Commissioner, Bajaur — dc-office@example.com. Privacy is covered separately in the Privacy Notice.
